2. Deleting your account

The iOS app provides an account-deletion flow that requests deletion from AMEN’s backend. If it fails or you cannot access your account, contact Email AMEN. We may ask for proportionate verification so an unauthorized person cannot delete or obtain your data. Do not send a password for verification.

Uninstalling the app alone does not delete backend records. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription: cancel it separately through Apple subscription cancellation. Device copies, exported content, backup expiry and records subject to lawful retention are distinct from removal of the active account.

3. Automatic renewal and cancellation

Auto-renewing subscriptions continue for the stated billing period until canceled under Apple’s applicable rules. Manage cancellation in your device’s Apple account subscription settings or follow Apple subscription cancellation. The subscription screen shows renewal dates and access after cancellation; store rules and mandatory law determine the result.

Deleting the app, deleting your AMEN account, signing out, emailing a cancellation request to AMEN or revoking permissions does not itself cancel an Apple-managed subscription. Price changes follow Apple’s notice and consent processes and applicable law. We do not claim a right to raise an existing price without required notice or agreement.

4. Refunds and purchase problems

For Apple-billed purchases, use Apple refund requests or reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund or investigate billing. Eligibility depends on the applicable store rules, circumstances and law. AMEN can help investigate access problems but cannot promise that Apple will grant every refund request.

This routing does not remove statutory remedies against a responsible trader. Report failures, duplicate access charges or a nonconforming service to us as well where appropriate. No clause here makes all purchases nonrefundable or requires you to abandon rights to repair, price reduction, termination or reimbursement.

5. Conversation history and deletion

Conversations, messages, responses and generated titles can be saved locally and in Supabase for continued access and context. Closing a conversation, closing the app or ending a session does not by itself delete saved history. This replaces any earlier description that chats are normally kept only for an active session.

Use available conversation controls or contact us to request deletion or a copy. A deletion request must also consider associated account records, service providers and backups; it does not automatically recall copies you exported or shared. Storage required for legal obligations or claims may continue with restricted use.
