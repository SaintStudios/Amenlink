Privacy policy

Amen is a Christian app for Bible reading, prayer and spiritual reflection. This policy applies to the amen app for Android. It explains how personal information is used in the app, including accounts, reading activity, Nema AI chat, audio, Bible Lock and subscriptions. The information processed depends on the features you choose.

Key points

  • Nema uses an enhanced Google AI model to provide AI chat. Your message and relevant conversation context are sent to Google to generate a response.
  • Amen creates a guest account without requiring an email address. You can save it with your Google account. Purchases are made and managed through Google Play.
  • Bible Lock checks on your phone which app is open so it can lock the apps you choose. This information is not sent to amen or to service providers.
  • Contact support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com for privacy requests. A Google Play subscription must be canceled in Google Play; deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

1. Privacy contact

For questions about how the amen app for Android uses personal information, contact support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com. You can use this address for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent and other privacy requests.

This policy describes processing associated with the amen app on Android devices. Google also processes information for its own services, such as Google Play, Google sign-in and Android, under its own privacy policy. Google's responsibilities do not replace the responsibilities associated with amen or reduce your rights.

2. Support and email correspondence

If you contact support, your email address, message, attachments and details needed to resolve the request are processed through the support email service. Please do not send passwords, complete payment details or sensitive information that is not needed to answer your question.

Article 6(1)(b) applies to requests about your contract or pre-contractual questions; Article 6(1)(f) applies to other correspondence and handling complaints. Relevant correspondence may be retained to resolve the issue, meet legal duties or establish and defend claims. Sensitive information requires an additional lawful condition.

3. Accounts, Google sign-in and profile

During onboarding, amen creates a guest account without requiring an email address. The account has a unique identifier and session information. Your chosen name and supported saved activity are associated with that account identifier. Guest access does not require verification of your real-world identity; account-linked information can still be personal data. Account identifiers, session information and preferences are stored with account services and on the device. Account information also includes creation and update times.

You can save your account with your Google account and later sign in with it again. Google confirms the sign-in and can provide an identifier, your name and your email address; amen does not receive your Google password and does not request access to other Google content. On Android, Google sign-in is the only way to recover an account. A guest account that has not been saved with Google cannot be recovered after signing out, reinstalling the app or changing devices. The usual bases are Article 6(1)(b), proportionate security under Article 6(1)(f), and legal duties under Article 6(1)(c).

4. Onboarding answers and faith personalization

Onboarding asks for your name and uses answers about faith goals, a faith vision, reading habits, obstacles, personal struggles, Bible version, denomination and gender to select relevant content and a read-aloud voice. These answers are stored on your device. Your name is saved with your account. Denomination is also saved with account chat settings and can be included in personalized Google AI requests.

These answers can reveal religious beliefs or personal circumstances and are covered by the sensitive-data section. Each purpose and recipient requires an appropriate lawful basis and, where applicable, an additional condition for sensitive data. Continuing onboarding, reading this policy or allowing a device permission does not by itself provide explicit consent for sensitive-data processing. Contact support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com about your answers, preferences or privacy requests.

5. Reading activity, highlights and personalization

Amen uses language, Bible-version choices, reading activity, streaks, achievements and settings to provide the selected features. On Android, verse highlights and the days on which your reading streak was active are saved on the device and synchronized with your account, so they are available when you sign in on another device. Saved chat messages, conversations, profile information and supported progress records also use cloud storage.

The purpose is to provide reading, progress and personalization functions, usually under Article 6(1)(b), with additional protection where information reveals beliefs or other sensitive circumstances. Use the relevant highlight, saved-message or conversation controls to manage saved content. Keep a separate copy of information important to you; this does not reduce your rights to a conforming service. Public profile and ranking visibility is explained separately below.

6. Public profile and leaderboards

Leaderboards display ranking information to other users, including your display name, profile picture where available, a country flag where available, reading streak, badges, score and rank. Supported progress records are synchronized with the ranking service to calculate and show these results. Profile and ranking information is therefore different from a private conversation.

The Android app does not use your location for leaderboards. Choose a name and photo suitable for this visibility and avoid confidential information in profile fields. Shared image links may also allow others to view the image. Ranking processing generally supports the feature under Article 6(1)(b), with additional protection for sensitive information. Contact amenapphilfe@gmail.com about displayed information or privacy requests.

7. Nema AI chat

Nema uses an enhanced Google AI model to provide AI chat. Your message and relevant conversation history are sent to Google to generate a response. Context may include your name, selected language and denomination when used for personalization. The conversation is generated by AI rather than a human adviser.

Google AI services also support Bible Lock reading recommendations and read-aloud audio. If you report an AI response, the selected response, the reason you choose, any details you add and references to the conversation are stored for review. Avoid sharing passwords, payment details or confidential information about another person. Requested features generally rely on Article 6(1)(b), with an additional lawful condition for sensitive personal data.

8. Google AI services

Google processes the information sent to its AI services to generate the requested answer. Its handling of inputs and responses depends on the applicable service terms and settings, including provisions concerning model improvement, safety and retention. Google's AI service terms are available at Google AI service terms.

Security, abuse prevention and legal obligations can involve temporary retention or review of AI requests. This policy does not promise that every provider record is deleted immediately. For Google's own privacy information, see Google privacy policy. You may contact support with questions about the handling of your chat information.

9. Conversation history and deletion

Conversations, messages, responses and titles may be saved locally and with your account so you can return to them. Closing a conversation or the app does not delete saved history. Saved messages and related account records can be separate from the conversation currently shown on screen.

Use available conversation controls or contact support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com to request deletion or a copy. Removal from active history, associated records, provider processing and backup expiry are separate parts of handling a request. Deletion does not automatically recall exported or shared copies. Records needed for legal obligations or claims may remain with restricted use.

10. Read-aloud audio

When you request read-aloud audio, the app sends the text to be read, its language and the selected voice to Google to generate speech. This can include Bible text, a devotional or a prayer. The generated audio is played on your device.

Read-aloud generates speech from text and does not record your microphone. Relevant text is processed to provide the requested audio; provider security and legal records may also apply. Playback generally relies on Article 6(1)(b), with additional protection if the text contains sensitive personal information.

11. Bible Lock on Android

Android has no Screen Time, so Bible Lock works differently than on iOS. It needs two settings that you turn on in Android settings: usage access, so the app can see which app is open, and display over other apps, so it can cover a locked app. While Bible Lock is active, a service runs in the foreground with a permanent notification, as Android requires. The apps you choose, schedules, unlock status and reading progress are stored only on your phone. Information about which apps you open is used on the device to apply the lock and is not sent to amen or to service providers.

You can withdraw usage access and display over other apps in Android settings at any time; Bible Lock then stops working. For an AI reading recommendation, your selected need, requested reading length, preferred language and recent chapter references can be sent to Google. Recent references help avoid repeated recommendations and are also stored on your phone. Processing generally provides the requested feature under Article 6(1)(b), with additional protection for sensitive information.

12. Profile photos and Android permissions

When you choose a profile photo, the app uses the Android photo picker. Amen receives only the image you select and does not request access to your photo library. The selected image is stored with the relevant account service and can appear with your profile or ranking information. Shared image links may give others access to that image.

Depending on the features you use, the app can ask for notification permission, usage access and display over other apps for Bible Lock, and permission to schedule exact reminders. You can manage these permissions in Android settings. Denying a permission can limit the related feature. A device permission is separate from consent to sensitive-data processing. Google's privacy information is available at Google privacy policy.

13. Notifications and reminders

Daily verse and streak reminders can be scheduled on your device. Where push notifications are available, the app registers a device push token with the notification service together with the platform, app language, time zone, reminder settings and your account identifier. Google delivers push notifications to Android devices.

You can change notification permission in Android settings. Revoking a permission does not itself establish that every previously registered token or service record has been deleted; contact amenapphilfe@gmail.com for deletion requests. A reminder does not cancel a subscription or replace the renewal information shown by Google Play. Google Play controls its own emails and notifications about subscriptions under Google privacy policy.

14. Days with Jesus and widgets

If you use Days with Jesus, the date you choose is saved on your device to calculate the day count. Android widgets use verses, content and preferences that the app saves on the device. You can remove widgets at any time on your home screen.

Widgets and notifications can show faith-related content on your home screen or lock screen. People who can see the screen may also see that content. Choose widgets and notification settings with this visibility in mind. Requested widget functions generally rely on Article 6(1)(b), with additional protection where information reveals religious beliefs or other sensitive data.

15. Comics, images and sharing

When you open comics or images in Discover, amen requests the content from delivery services and can keep temporary copies for display. Delivery involves the requested file and ordinary network connection information, including the IP address used for the connection.

Content you share through Android's share function goes to the app or person you choose. Shared copies are separate from account records and are not automatically recalled by account deletion. Media processing generally provides your requested feature under Article 6(1)(b).

16. Purchases and Google Play

Google Play handles payments and billing for purchases in the Android app. Amen does not receive your full payment-card number. A purchase validation service uses your account identifier, transaction details, products, trial status and subscription access information to recognize your purchases. Restoring purchases links the purchases of your Google Play account to your current amen account. Google Play's terms are available at Google Play terms of service.

The Android app does not use purchase information for advertising. Access administration generally relies on Article 6(1)(b) and legally required records on Article 6(1)(c). Google's handling of purchase information is described in Google privacy policy.

17. Analytics and advertising measurement

The current Android app does not include third-party advertising measurement or attribution tools and does not request access to the Android advertising ID. It does not report installations, trial starts or purchases to advertising partners. The advertising measurement described in the iOS privacy policy does not apply to the Android app.

Google Play may provide amen with statistics and crash reports about the app, depending on your device and Google account settings. These reports help find technical faults and are handled under Google privacy policy. We will update this policy if measurement in the Android app changes.

18. Religious beliefs and other sensitive data

Denomination, prayer, faith goals and conversations may reveal religious beliefs, health or other special categories of data under Article 9 GDPR. Such processing needs both an Article 6 basis and an applicable Article 9 condition, ordinarily explicit consent for optional faith personalization. Reading a policy, using the app or allowing an Android permission is not that explicit consent.

You may request withdrawal, restriction or deletion at amenapphilfe@gmail.com and change available preferences. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. Avoid entering sensitive data about other people without authority. This document cannot create consent or make an otherwise unlawful disclosure lawful.

19. Children and parental authorization

Use of amen must comply with applicable child-data rules, legally effective parental authorization where required, and the conditions of external services. A Google Play content rating does not replace these requirements. Google's AI service conditions are linked in this policy and remain relevant to features that use those services.

Where consent is needed, the requirements for children depend on local law. Contact support if you believe a child's information was processed without the necessary authorization. We will investigate the request and arrange the restriction or deletion required in the circumstances.

20. Purposes and legal bases

The relevant bases depend on the purpose: Article 6(1)(b) for requested account, reading, AI and purchase-access services; Article 6(1)(c) for legal duties; Article 6(1)(f) for proportionate security, service operation and handling non-contractual correspondence; Article 6(1)(a) for processing requiring consent.

Device-storage rules may also require consent unless access is strictly necessary. Special-category data additionally needs an Article 9 condition. We cannot replace consent-required advertising with a blanket legitimate-interest claim. Required account information is needed for the relevant service; optional information and permissions can be declined, with possible limits to their features.

21. Service providers and other recipients

Recipient categories include providers of account authentication, cloud hosting and storage, purchase validation, push notifications and support email. Google provides AI services, Google sign-in, Google Play purchases and push notification delivery. Information shared depends on the feature and purpose. The Android app does not share information with advertising partners.

Necessary information may also be shared with authorized support personnel, professional advisers or authorities where legally required. Processing and any international transfers remain subject to applicable safeguards. Contact amenapphilfe@gmail.com for more information about recipients and arrangements relevant to your information.

22. International transfers

Providers can operate or give authorized support access outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Server region, support access and further subprocessors must all be considered; choosing an EU database region does not by itself rule out every international transfer.

Where GDPR transfer rules apply, a transfer needs an applicable safeguard, such as an adequacy decision covering the recipient or appropriate contractual safeguards, including standard contractual clauses and any necessary additional measures. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data. We do not promise universal EU-only storage.

23. Retention criteria and backups

Account and synchronized content are retained to provide your account and chosen features until removed or no longer needed. Information stored only on your device stays there until you delete it, delete your account or remove the app. The Android app excludes its data from Android cloud backups and device transfers. Support records depend on the request and legal obligations. Transaction records, fraud evidence and disputed matters may need longer retention under applicable law.

After account deletion, deleted data can remain in encrypted server backups until they expire, at most 7 days later; it is not restored from them. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and the purchase validation service under an anonymous identifier, not your name, for as long as billing, tax and fraud-prevention law requires. Cloud and AI services otherwise retain information according to the relevant purpose, agreements and legal duties.

24. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under You → Delete Account. The account is deleted immediately, you are signed out and the account's data on your phone is removed. Deletion cannot be undone. If you cannot use the app, request deletion at amenapphilfe@gmail.com; the steps are described on Delete your account. Proportionate identity verification may be needed; you will not be asked to send a password.

Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account. Removing amen's access in your Google account settings is also separate from deleting information held by amen. Neither app removal nor account deletion cancels a Google Play subscription: cancel it separately, see Google Play subscription cancellation. Copies you shared outside the app and records subject to lawful retention are distinct from removal of the active account.

25. Your rights and complaints

Where the GDPR applies, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction and, where the conditions are met, portability. You may withdraw consent and object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation. You may object to direct marketing at any time.

Send requests to amenapphilfe@gmail.com. We normally respond within one month; legally permitted extensions require notice and reasons. Requests are normally free, subject to statutory exceptions. You may complain to the supervisory authority where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. Contacting amen first is not a condition for complaining.

26. Security and automated processing

Data transfers use encrypted connections where supported, with access controls and other technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the processing. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and account, and report suspected unauthorized access.

AI generates responses and personalization, and automated checks can manage access or purchase entitlement. These functions are not presented as decisions about legal rights, credit, employment or medical treatment. If you believe an automated result significantly affects you, contact us for review and the protections applicable to that decision.

27. Changes and related documents

The date and version at the top identify this notice. We will update it when services, recipients or legal requirements change and provide additional notice where required. Publishing a revised notice does not authorize a new purpose that requires consent.

The Terms of use explain use of the app and purchases. Updates to this policy do not reduce mandatory privacy rights. Contact support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com if you have questions about this notice or how your information is used.
