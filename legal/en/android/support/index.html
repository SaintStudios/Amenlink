Key points

  • Contact support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com for privacy requests. A Google Play subscription must be canceled in Google Play; deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.
  • Before you confirm a purchase, Google Play shows the price, billing period, any free trial and the product you are buying.

1. Contact support

Contact amen support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com for app help, account questions, subscription access or complaints. Google Play's linked guidance explains its billing and cancellation processes. For personal-information requests, see Privacy policy.

Include the product or feature concerned and a concise description. Share an order reference only when needed and avoid passwords, full card details and unnecessary private conversation content. These contact instructions do not restrict legally valid notices or require use of a particular channel where the law permits another.

2. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under You → Delete Account. The account is deleted immediately, you are signed out and the account's data on your phone is removed. Deletion cannot be undone. If you cannot use the app, request deletion at amenapphilfe@gmail.com; the steps are described on Delete your account. Proportionate identity verification may be needed; you will not be asked to send a password.

Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account. Removing amen's access in your Google account settings is also separate from deleting information held by amen. Neither app removal nor account deletion cancels a Google Play subscription: cancel it separately, see Google Play subscription cancellation. Copies you shared outside the app and records subject to lawful retention are distinct from removal of the active account.

3. Automatic renewal and cancellation

Auto-renewing subscriptions continue for the stated billing period until canceled under Google Play's rules. Cancel in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions or follow Google Play subscription cancellation. After cancellation, access normally continues until the end of the paid period; Google Play shows the relevant dates, and mandatory law determines the result.

Deleting the app, deleting your amen account, signing out, emailing a cancellation request to amen or revoking permissions does not itself cancel a Google Play subscription. Price changes follow Google Play's notice and consent processes and applicable law. We do not claim a right to raise an existing price without required notice or agreement.

4. Refunds and purchase problems

For Google Play purchases, contact amen support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com or see Google Play refunds. Google Play's policies, the circumstances and applicable law determine whether a refund is granted. We review each request individually and cannot promise that every request will be granted.

This routing does not remove statutory remedies against a responsible trader. Report failures, duplicate access charges or a nonconforming service to us as well where appropriate. No clause here makes all purchases nonrefundable or requires you to abandon rights to repair, price reduction, termination or reimbursement.

5. Conversation history and deletion

Conversations, messages, responses and titles may be saved locally and with your account so you can return to them. Closing a conversation or the app does not delete saved history. Saved messages and related account records can be separate from the conversation currently shown on screen.

Use available conversation controls or contact support at amenapphilfe@gmail.com to request deletion or a copy. Removal from active history, associated records, provider processing and backup expiry are separate parts of handling a request. Deletion does not automatically recall exported or shared copies. Records needed for legal obligations or claims may remain with restricted use.
